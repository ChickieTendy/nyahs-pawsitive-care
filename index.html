Meet Nyah

Thoughtful care starts with trust.

Hi, I’m Nyah! I started Nyah’s Pawsitive Care because of my love for animals and my experience as a pet owner. I’m currently a Veterinary Technician student, and I also work at DogDrop, a dog daycare and overnight care facility. Between school, my work experience, and caring for my own dog, I’m continuing to build my knowledge and hands-on experience with animal care, health, behavior, routines, and well-being.

I understand how much trust it takes to leave your pet with someone else. My goal is to provide the patience, attention, comfort, and love I would want for my own dog. I respect each pet’s routine, personality, and individual needs so they can feel safe and comfortable while their family is away.

To me, “Same Love, Different Address” means caring for every pet like family and giving pet parents peace of mind.

Frequently Asked Questions

What pet parents may want to know.

Does a submitted request guarantee my reservation?

No. Every request is reviewed for availability, care needs, and fit before a reservation is confirmed.

Where do you provide services?

Nyah's Pawsitive Care serves Fort Lauderdale, Florida and surrounding areas. Final availability may depend on the requested location and dates.

What information should I provide?

Please share the requested dates, service, pet details, routine, health or medication needs, relevant behavior information, and current contact information.

How are prices confirmed?

Pricing should be confirmed after the request details are reviewed. This Version 1 site intentionally does not take automatic payments.
